## Notes (D1 final)

- agensio is ahead of nginx in every row: 13 % less CPU per request on the small JSON
  answer at 64 connections (4.98 vs 5.70 us, 201k vs 176k req/s), 10 % at 16 connections,
  46 % less on the 100 KB body, 9 % less on the 20 ms application at 256 connections.
  Both reuse upstream connections the same way.
- The path from the first agensio rows (`proxy-20260917-173601.md`, 6.2 us) to these:
  strace showed 7 syscalls per proxied request against the static path's 2; two
  `timerfd_settime` (an Asio timer armed per exchange phase) and one `epoll_ctl` (a
  readiness wait) were overhead. A per-pool 250 ms deadline tick replaced the timers and
  the speculative read came back. 4 syscalls per request now: recvfrom and sendmsg on the
  client side, sendto and recvfrom on the upstream side.
- Earlier files: `proxy-20260917-172009.md` (FastCGI-sized pool defaults, 503s at 256
  connections), `proxy-20260917-172825.md` (before inline upstream completions).
