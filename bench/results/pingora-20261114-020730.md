# agensio and Pingora as reverse proxies, 20261114-020730

- machine: x86_64, AMD Ryzen 9 9900X 12-Core Processor, 24 CPUs, Linux 6.12.116+deb13-amd64
- commit: 4dc6d338+dirty; command: `bench/pingora/run.sh -T memory`
- agensio: agensio 0.1.0-alpha.146, OpenSSL 3.5.7 from the system
- Pingora: pingora 0.9.1 (bench/pingora/proxy, release build with LTO), its own OpenSSL 3.6.3 linked in (the crate's `openssl` feature)
- origin: build/agensio_upstream, 4 workers; load: wrk debian/4.1.0-4+b1, h2load nghttp2/1.64.0, 10 s a row, 1 round(s), 4 load threads (8 with more than one proxy worker)
- pinned: the proxy on CPUs 0..W-1 (W workers or threads), the origin on 4-7, the load generator on 8-11 and 20-23
- both forward Host, X-Forwarded-For, X-Forwarded-Proto and X-Real-IP, keep connections to the origin, write no access log; agensio `workers = W`, Pingora `threads = W` (work stealing on, its default)
- CPU us/req = CPU time of the proxy's process (all threads) / requests answered; RSS after the row; origin conns = connections the proxy opened to origins during the row
