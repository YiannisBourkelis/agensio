## Notes (D0 baseline, the targets for D1-D4)

- The upstream alone does 470-510k req/s on one core at 2 us per request, so both
  proxies are the bottleneck in every row: nginx caps at 180k (5.5 us per request), Caddy
  at 40k (25 us). The 100 KB body costs nginx 33 us and Caddy 49 us per request; the
  20 ms application at 256 connections costs nginx 8 us and Caddy 36 us.
- Connection reuse: nginx with `keepalive 64` and `keepalive_requests 1000000` in the
  upstream block opens 1-2 connections for a whole run (248 for the 256-connection slow
  case, as expected). nginx's default `keepalive_requests` of 1000 opened 1.7k per run;
  the tuned setting is what the script uses. Caddy opens thousands (its pool is bounded
  by idle-connection limits and closes under load) at 64 connections and above.
- agensio's target: the nginx row in every case, and connection reuse like nginx's.
