## Notes (D1, first agensio rows)

- agensio is 9 % behind nginx on the small JSON answer (6.2 vs 5.7 us per request), 38 %
  ahead on the 100 KB body (20.9 vs 33.9 us, the buffered body goes out in one writev),
  level on the 20 ms application at 256 connections, and reuses upstream connections the
  same way (one per run; the slow case opens up to the concurrency, as nginx does).
- Before the two fixes in this commit (inline upstream completions, readiness wait
  instead of a speculative first recv) agensio was at 6.98 us and 143k req/s on JSON
  (`proxy-20260917-172825.md`); the first run with FastCGI-sized pool defaults answered
  the 256-connection case with 503s (`proxy-20260917-172009.md`).
- What remains on the small-answer path (roadmap D7): three timer arms per exchange,
  allocations for the Exchange, the HttpRequest, the completion std::function and the
  forwarded head string.
