## Notes (Node.js origin, D7)

- A single Node.js hello-world process is the bottleneck at about 156-160k req/s (6.3 us
  of its own CPU per request when hit by wrk directly), so both proxies are near it and
  the row compares what each proxy costs the origin, not the proxy's own CPU (nginx 6.0-6.1
  us, agensio 5.7-5.9 us per request).
- agensio 153-155k req/s vs nginx 159-161k: the gap was 6-7 % (`proxy-20260917-204812.md`)
  before the redundant `Connection: keep-alive` was dropped from kept origin connections
  (HTTP/1.1 is persistent by default) and is 3-4 % now. What remains is the two extra
  fields agensio always sends and this nginx configuration does not, X-Forwarded-Proto and
  X-Forwarded-Host: Node parses them on every request. They stay; a proxy that lets the
  application know the scheme and host it was reached on is the point of the defaults.
